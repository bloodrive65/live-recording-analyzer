## What's new in 1.4.4

- **The Analyzer tab has four sections:**
  1. **Analyze your last recording.** It finds your newest recording by itself and shows its details. Nothing is analyzed until you click the button, unless you turned on automatic analysis in Settings.
  2. **Analyze a recording from an OBS log.** Pick any log, tick recordings, and click Analyze selected.
  3. **Analyze a different video (new).** Browse to any video on any drive. If it's listed in one of your OBS logs, it gets the same full analysis as your last recording. Otherwise the file is checked by itself. The app tells you which before you start and shows a time estimate.
  4. **Last report**, unchanged.
- **The OBS Reports tab only lists files.** It never analyzes anything. **OBS log files** lists every log OBS has written: when OBS was opened, when the log was last written, the number of recordings, and whether it was analyzed. The list updates by itself while the page is open. Use **Analyze on the Analyzer tab** to open a log there, or **Open log file** to view it. **Analyzed reports** lists every report, with the video, the result and the OBS log it came from.
- **Faster lists.** The app skims logs in the background instead of reading them in full. In testing, 300 logs (53 MB) loaded in under 0.2 seconds.

Includes everything from 1.4.3.
