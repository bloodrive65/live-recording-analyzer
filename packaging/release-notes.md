## What's new in 1.3.0

- **Analyzer tab**: shows your latest recording and analyzes it with one click. Nothing opens by itself any more, so the window no longer flickers or minimizes.
- **Report stays available**: the last result and its Open report button stay on the Analyzer tab, even after you restart the app, until you analyze again.
- **Online report page**: Open report shows the report on the Live Recording Analyzer website. The report is stored inside the link itself (nothing is uploaded), so it works on any device. Copy link lets you share it. Open saved copy still works offline.
- **OBS Reports tab** (was Reports): all saved reports, plus analyzing older recordings from any OBS log.
- **Choose automatic or manual analysis** in the last step of the guided setup.
- **Guided setup question** now appears every time the app opens unless you tick Remember my choice.
- **Built-in updater**: checks for new versions, shows what's new, then downloads, installs and reopens the app.
- New app icon.

Download **Live-Recording-Analyzer-Setup-*.exe** below and run it. Your sessions, reports and settings are kept. The portable .zip runs without installing.
