How it helps

Before, during and after every recording

You don't have to watch the app. It runs quietly in the background and only speaks up when something actually matters.

BEFORE

Check OBS settings

One click reads your OBS profile, scenes and last log, and lists anything that will cause trouble:

  • Frame-rate mismatches between your capture source and OBS
  • Color range, color format and encoder bit-depth conflicts
  • HDR screen captures without Force SDR
  • A slow, external or nearly full recording drive
DURING

Live Advisor

Checks once per second and sends a Windows pop-up when there's a problem:

  • Skipped and dropped frames, with the likely cause at that moment (GPU, encoder, CPU, drive, temperature)
  • Drive stalls, low free space, a file that stopped growing
  • Audio delays and buffering changes
  • Checks the file itself 25 seconds in, so you can fix mistakes early
AFTER

Report with timestamps

When you stop recording it analyzes the recording on its own:

  • Every problem with the clock time and the position in the video
  • OBS settings compared to what the file really contains
  • Optional deep scan for repeated or frozen frames
  • Windows events such as GPU driver resets or USB disconnects
Lightweight

Made to stay out of your game's way

Its background work runs at low priority and reads the counters Windows already keeps, about once a second. It doesn't capture your screen, inject into games or hook into OBS. The status bar shows how much CPU and memory the app itself uses, so you can check for yourself. The heavier deep scan only runs when you ask for it, and it also runs at low priority.

Safe by design

Recommends, never changes

The app never changes your OBS settings. It tells you exactly where each setting lives in OBS and what to choose. The only thing it can do in OBS is pause or resume your recording, and only when you click that button. Everything stays on your PC.

Start in three steps

Up and running in a few minutes

  1. Install it. Download the installer from the latest release and run it. Windows may warn you because the app isn't digitally signed yet. Here's why it's safe and how to continue.
  2. Let the guided setup check your PC. The first time you open the app, it asks if you'd like guided setup. It finds OBS, helps you turn on the connection it needs, and checks your recording drive and graphics card.
  3. Click “Start watching,” then play. Record in OBS as you normally would. If something goes wrong you'll get a pop-up, and when you stop recording a report appears in the app.
